[Codevent](https://cc.cucats.org/about) is our December puzzle competition, built along the lines of Advent of Code. A problem goes up every day at 12:00 UTC. Solve it and you get a star.

The scoring is what makes it a competition. Your score for each day depends on how many people solved it before you did, so the same correct answer is worth more at 12:05 than at 23:00. Over a month that turns into a leaderboard where consistency and speed both count.

## Why it suits Cambridge

December is mostly vacation, which is the point. Michaelmas has finished, you are at home, and a puzzle a day is about the right size to fit around everything else. Plenty of people who never enter a timed contest during term will happily do Codevent from their bedroom.

It also pairs well with Part IA Algorithms. The puzzles reward recognising a standard technique quickly, which is the same skill the course builds. Doing both in the same year is a good combination.

## Getting started

You need no particular setup. Read the problem, write something that produces the right answer, submit it. Any language works, since what you submit is the answer and never the source.

For anyone new to this kind of puzzle:

- Read the whole problem before writing anything. The second half often changes the approach the first half suggested.
- Write the obvious slow solution first. Most days it finishes fast enough, and it doubles as a check on anything cleverer you write later.
- Keep your input in a file, so that part two does not have you re-pasting it. You will thank yourself.
- Do not chase the leaderboard on day one. The people at the top are up at midday with a template ready, and treating it as a race against them takes the fun out of it quickly.

Questions and problems go to the CUCaTS Discord, which is also where most of the December chat happens. Prizes are announced alongside the competition each year.

If you enjoy it, [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/) itself runs at the same time, and the [Competitive Programming](/wiki/tutorials/competitive-programming) page covers where to go next.
