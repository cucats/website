Three events take up most of our year: a hackathon in Michaelmas, a puzzle competition through December, and a game jam in Lent. Each has its own page here with the detail that does not fit on a poster.

Around those we run talks, workshops, socials and formals. Those get announced on the [mailing list](https://lists.cam.ac.uk/sympa/subscribe/soc-cucats-events) and Discord as they come up, and they live on the [events calendar](/events), since the dates move every year.

Everything below is open to current Cambridge students. Most of it is free, which is down to our [sponsors](/wiki/sponsors).

## The big three

[Cam Hack](/wiki/events/cam-hack) is a weekend hackathon where teams build whatever they like to a theme. The 2025 edition ran to "Unintended Behaviour", and the projects were as silly as that suggests.

[Cambridge Game Jam](/wiki/events/cambridge-game-jam) gives you about 48 hours and a surprise theme to make a game. Video games, board games and ARGs have all been entered.

[CUCaTS Codevent](/wiki/events/codevent) runs through December, with a programming puzzle released daily and a leaderboard that rewards solving early.

## Running your own

If you want to put on an event, CUCaTS can fund it and help with the logistics. Past member-led events have ranged from picnics to contests, so the bar is lower than people assume. The [event funding guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WQdX0mt8jfy-CM6IOvB9yNzkG19yL9MsVQizpaagKuE/edit?usp=sharing) explains what to put in a proposal, and [Getting Started](/wiki/getting-started) covers who to talk to.

## Other societies

We co-run things with [CyberSoc](https://cybersoc.soc.srcf.net) on security and [UCCPS](https://uccps.soc.srcf.net) on competitive programming. Their announcement channels are worth joining alongside ours if either area appeals.
