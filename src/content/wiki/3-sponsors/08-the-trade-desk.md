The Trade Desk runs a demand-side platform for digital advertising, describing itself as the leading independent DSP built for data-driven marketers. Brands and agencies use it to buy advertising across display, video, audio, connected TV, digital out-of-home, mobile and native formats.

The platform connects to hundreds of data, inventory and industry partners, offers more than 100,000 pre-vetted third-party data segments, and reaches hundreds of millions of connected TV households. It operates globally, with a UK presence alongside offices across the Americas, Europe and Asia.

## The engineering problem

Advertising technology gets overlooked by students, which is a shame, because the systems problem is genuinely hard. A bid request arrives, and the platform has to decide whether to bid and how much, within a budget measured in tens of milliseconds, millions of times per second, while respecting the targeting and spending rules of every campaign involved.

That is a real-time distributed systems problem with a hard latency ceiling, closer to the trading firms elsewhere on this list than the phrase "adtech" suggests.
