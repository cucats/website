Sponsorship pays for our events. It is why Cam Hack feeds 250 people for a weekend, why the game jam has prizes, and why almost everything we run costs students nothing.

The arrangement is straightforward on both sides. Firms sponsor us because they want to meet Cambridge students, and we run talks, workshops and dinners where that happens. Nobody is obliged to attend any of it, and plenty of members never go to a sponsor event.

These pages describe each sponsor and what they offer. The [sponsors page](/sponsors) on the main site has the same list with logos and links.

## Making use of them

Sponsor events are the easiest recruiting route most students will get, and they are underused by people who assume they need to be in third year first. A few notes worth having:

Turn up in first year even though you are not applying for anything. The point is finding out what these firms actually do, which is hard to work out from a careers page. Trading, infrastructure and product work are different jobs, and the descriptions blur together until you have spoken to someone doing them.

Applications open earlier than feels reasonable. Summer internships often open the previous September or October, and popular schemes close before Christmas. If you wait until Easter you will find the good ones gone.

Ask the engineers as well as the recruiters. The recruiters are there to be helpful and will answer process questions well. The engineers will tell you what the work is like on a bad week, which is more useful.

The [University Careers Service](https://www.careers.cam.ac.uk/) covers everything outside our own sponsors, and will review a CV for free.
