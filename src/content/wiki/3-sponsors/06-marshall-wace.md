Marshall Wace is a provider of alternative investment solutions, operating as a single team across its global offices. The firm describes a culture built on continuous innovation and doing things differently, with the aim of creating proprietary value for clients.

Technology sits at the centre of the business. Marshall Wace invests continuously in its systems, data, infrastructure and people, and treats staying ahead on technology as a competitive advantage in itself. Staff are expected to look for ways to automate and simplify complex processes, and to push improvements to the platform without waiting to be asked.

## The technology internship

Marshall Wace runs a dedicated [technology internship programme](https://www.mwam.com/technology-internship-programme), which is the main route in for students who want to build systems. Worth knowing about, since at most firms in this space the engineering and investment paths recruit separately.
