Qube Research & Technologies, usually shortened to QRT, is a global investment manager running a range of strategies across geographies, asset classes and time frames.

In its own description, combining data, research, technology and trading expertise has shaped a collaborative approach to solving hard problems, with a culture of innovation aimed at delivering returns for investors.

## What that means for a student

QRT sits in the quantitative investment category alongside several other CUCaTS sponsors, and the graduate routes split roughly into research, trading and engineering. Research leans on statistics and machine learning. Engineering covers the systems that move and process the data those strategies need, which is a large software problem in its own right.

If the distinction between these roles is unclear, that is normal and worth asking about at a sponsor event. Firms in this space use the same job titles for quite different work.

## With CUCaTS

QRT is a **Gold** sponsor, and has backed [Cam Hack](/wiki/events/cam-hack) and [Cambridge Game Jam](/wiki/events/cambridge-game-jam).
