Anthropic is an AI safety and research company. It builds Claude, a large language model available through a chat interface, an API, and a set of developer tools, and it publishes research on both the capabilities and the risks of these systems.

The company describes safety as a systematic science, feeding research results back into the products and publishing much of the work openly. Its stated aim is to start a race to the top on safety, where firms compete on how trustworthy their systems are. It operates as a Public Benefit Corporation, with a Long-Term Benefit Trust sitting alongside the usual shareholder governance.

## Why students end up interested

The research is unusually accessible. Interpretability work, evaluations and alignment papers get published in a form you can read without a PhD, and a fair number of Cambridge students have worked through them long before applying to anything.

The engineering side is less visible and just as substantial: serving these models at scale, building the tooling around them, and the infrastructure underneath. Not all of it is machine learning research.
