TPP is a global digital health company. Over 7,000 organisations use its software to care for more than 50 million patients, across GPs, emergency departments, hospitals, mental health services and social care. The effect is that wherever a patient turns up, a detailed and current record is available.

Its database is one of the largest in the world, processing a billion transactions a day, which TPP notes is more than the London Stock Exchange and Visa combined.

## Why it appeals to this particular audience

TPP asks specifically for students interested in functional programming, compilers, programming languages and distributed systems. That is an unusual advert, and it lands well in Cambridge, where Part IA starts you in OCaml and Part IB covers compiler construction and semantics.

It is also worth knowing about as a counterexample to the assumption that this kind of work only exists in finance. The problems are of a similar difficulty and the thing being built is a health record system used across the country.

TPP offers internships, summer projects and PhD positions.
