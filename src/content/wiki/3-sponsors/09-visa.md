Visa is a world leader in digital payments, handling more than 276 billion payment transactions a year between consumers, merchants, financial institutions and governments across over 200 countries and territories. Its stated mission is to connect the world through a convenient, reliable and secure payments network so that individuals, businesses and economies can thrive.

On joining, Visa describes a culture of purpose and belonging, where growth is a priority and identity is embraced, and makes the point that economies including everyone everywhere lift everyone everywhere. Work there touches billions of people, largely through widening access to money movement.

## Scale as the interesting part

That transaction figure works out to thousands of payments every second, sustained, worldwide, with correctness that cannot be negotiated away. A payments network has no acceptable failure mode where a transaction is quietly lost or applied twice.

Systems built to that standard are a good thing to have seen early. Consistency guarantees, fraud detection at scale and settlement across hundreds of currencies are all live engineering problems here, and they map onto Part IB material more directly than most industry work does.
