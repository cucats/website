Citadel is one of the world's largest alternative investment firms. Ken Griffin founded it in 1990, a year after graduating, on the idea that talented people with strong quantitative analytics and good software could find opportunities in capital markets. It now runs over $65 billion in investment capital with a team of around 3,300.

The firm hires across an unusually wide range of academic backgrounds, bringing together PhDs from more than 60 fields, from aeronautics and atmospheric science through to computer engineering and statistics.

## Citadel and Citadel Securities

These are two different firms and the distinction confuses people every recruiting season. Citadel is the investment firm described above. Citadel Securities, established by Griffin and his partners in 2002, is a market maker and one of the largest liquidity providers in the world, serving more than 1,600 institutional clients including central banks and sovereign wealth funds.

They recruit separately. Check which one a given internship belongs to before you apply, because the work differs.
