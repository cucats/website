incident.io builds the tooling engineering teams use when something breaks in production. It describes itself as the single place you go when things go wrong, helping teams respond to incidents, cut downtime, and learn from what happened afterwards. The company has raised a $62m Series B, and its customers include OpenAI, Lovable and Netflix.

## A different shape of employer

Most of our sponsors are large financial firms. incident.io is a startup, which changes the job itself and not simply the size of the office. You will touch more of the stack, ship faster, and have far less process between you and production.

It is also a rare case where the product is something you will use on the other side. Anyone who ends up on call at any company will meet incident tooling, so the domain makes sense long before you have industry experience.
