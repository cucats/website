Jane Street is a research-driven trading firm. It commits its own capital across global markets and describes itself as technical to the core, with work spanning low-latency networking, compilers and distributed systems. Machine learning has become central to how it trades.

## The OCaml connection

Jane Street runs almost its entire codebase in OCaml, which makes it the largest industrial user of the language by some distance. It maintains a good deal of the open-source ecosystem around it, including the Core standard library and the Async concurrency library, and Yaron Minsky from the firm co-wrote [Real World OCaml](https://dev.realworldocaml.org/).

That is a pleasant coincidence for anyone in Part IA, since Foundations of Computer Science is taught in OCaml. The language you meet in your first term at Cambridge is the one this firm writes production trading systems in, which is a rarer overlap than it sounds. Our [Setting Up OCaml](/wiki/tutorials/ocaml-setup) page covers getting an environment working.

## INSIGHT

Jane Street runs INSIGHT, a multi-day programme for women, transgender and gender-expansive students. It covers the roles inside the firm and the specific concepts and tools used day to day, across three tracks: software engineering, trading, and strategy and product. Topics and locations vary by event.
